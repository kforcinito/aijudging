Work stream 1

Overview

Judges spend part of every routine looking down to write, tally, or enter scores, and that is time they are not watching the athletes. Eyes Up Judging moves that busywork to an AI assistant so judges can stay focused on the floor while the record of what they saw is captured accurately.

How it works

Capture

Hands-free input

Judges note deductions, skills, and observations by voice or with simple one-touch input while they keep watching. The assistant timestamps each note against the routine.

Assemble

Live score sheet

The AI maps every note to the official rubric categories, adds up the totals, and flags missing or conflicting entries before the score is submitted.

Confirm

Judge sign-off

At the end of the routine the judge reviews the assembled sheet, makes corrections, and submits. Nothing goes to the tabulation room until the judge approves it.

Judge stays in control: Eyes Up is an assistant, not a scorer. It records and organizes what the judge observes, and every submitted score belongs to the judge.

What success looks like

  • Judges spend more of each routine watching the floor.
  • Fewer arithmetic, transcription, and missing-entry errors reach tabulation.
  • Faster turnaround from the end of a routine to a submitted score.
  • A clear, timestamped record that supports score reviews and coach feedback.
