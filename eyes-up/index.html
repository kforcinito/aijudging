Work stream 1

Overview

Judges spend part of every routine looking down to write comments, and that is time they are not watching the athletes. With Eyes Up, judges speak their observations hands-free. An AI assistant turns them into streamlined, refined comments that the judge reviews, edits, and copies and pastes into SimpleScore.

How it works

Capture

Hands-free voice input

Judges speak their observations out loud while they keep watching the routine. No writing, no looking down.

Refine

Coach-ready comments

The AI turns the judge's spoken observations into clean, streamlined comments, keeping the judge's own words and meaning.

Confirm

Judge review

The judge reviews and edits the refined comments, then copies and pastes them into SimpleScore. Nothing is entered until the judge approves it.

Judge stays in control: Eyes Up is an assistant, not a scorer. AI refines the wording, but every comment and score belongs to the judge.

What success looks like

  • Judges spend more of each routine watching the floor.
  • Clearer, more consistent comments for coaches.
  • Faster turnaround from the end of a routine to comments entered in SimpleScore.
